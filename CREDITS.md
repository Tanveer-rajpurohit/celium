# Credits & Attribution

Celium was inspired by and builds upon foundational ideas originated by the CloudStream open-source project and its community of extension developers and content providers.

- **Foundational Engine**: CloudStream (GPL-3.0) — media center engine and modular extension architecture concepts.
- **Provider Community**: Extension repositories and scraper architectures provided by community maintainers (Phisher, CNC, CSX, CXXX, GizliKeyif).

Celium is an independently developed media application featuring custom Jetpack Compose UI, multi-tier feed caching, concurrent provider loading, and smart content classification.
